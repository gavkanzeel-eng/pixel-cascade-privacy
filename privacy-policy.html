Pixel Cascade — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 06.11.2026

This policy explains how Pixel Cascade Studio (public brand; legally responsible operator: Oleksandr Starovoit) ("we", "us") processes information in the Android mobile puzzle game Pixel Cascade. Contact us at gavkan.zeel@gmail.com. Legal service address: Bodelschwinghstr. 59, 92224 Amberg, Germany.

1. Information and game progress

The game stores scores, saved rounds, settings and advertising-frequency information locally on the device, using Unity's local storage (PlayerPrefs). In the current version there is no game account or cloud synchronization of this progress. You can generally remove locally stored progress through Android's app-data controls or by uninstalling the app. Clearing app data also resets local settings and progress.

2. Advertising and third-party services

Pixel Cascade uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) for optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads. Google and its advertising partners may automatically process information including IP address (which can indicate approximate location), advertising/device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and device diagnostics. They use this information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalization. The categories and purposes may vary with user choices, region and SDK configuration.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

3. Consent and privacy choices

Where required by applicable law, an in-app consent message is displayed through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before advertisements are requested. You can review or change advertising privacy choices using Settings → Ad Privacy Choices whenever that option is required and available in the app. Declining personalized advertising consent may change which ads are eligible to be served, but does not necessarily disable all ads.

4. Purposes and legal bases

We store local gameplay information to provide saved games, settings and personal best records. To the extent this activity involves personal data under the GDPR, we rely on our legitimate interest in providing reliable game functionality (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where advertising technology requires consent, including for personalized advertising and access to or storage of information on a device, we rely on consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR together with applicable ePrivacy rules). You may withdraw consent through the available privacy choices. Limited advertising, security and fraud-prevention activities may rely on other applicable legal grounds where permitted; Google and its advertising partners explain their independent data processing in their linked privacy notices.

5. Data sharing, transfers and retention

Advertising data may be processed by Google and its service providers/advertising partners, including in countries outside your country of residence. Google's applicable notices describe its data handling and safeguards. We retain game data on the device until you delete the app's stored data; Google's retention periods are governed by its own policies and settings. We do not operate a separate server storing your saved game in the current version.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request information, access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or portability, and to withdraw consent where relevant. Contact gavkan.zeel@gmail.com for requests about our processing. You may also contact the relevant data protection supervisory authority. Advertising preferences managed by Google can also be reviewed through the consent interface and Google settings.

7. Intended age group (13+)

Pixel Cascade is intended for players aged 13 and older, including teenagers and adults. The game is not designed for children under 13. It has no player accounts, in-game chat, or fields for entering a name or email address. The current app does not verify a player's age. Parents and guardians can contact us using the contact address above with questions about privacy and the use of the app by younger teenagers.

8. Updates

We may update this policy as features or data processing practices change. We will publish the revised policy with an updated effective date.

1. Spielstände und Einstellungen

Punktestände, gespeicherte Partien, Einstellungen und Informationen zur Häufigkeit von Werbeeinblendungen werden lokal auf dem Gerät mit Unity PlayerPrefs gespeichert. Die aktuelle Version hat keine Spielerkonten und synchronisiert die Spielstände nicht mit einem Cloud-Dienst. Lokale Daten können in der Regel über die Android-App-Dateneinstellungen oder durch Deinstallation gelöscht werden; dadurch gehen Spielstände und Einstellungen verloren.

Pixel Cascade verwendet das Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) für freiwillige Werbung gegen Spielvorteile und gelegentliche Vollbildanzeigen. Google und Werbepartner können unter anderem IP-Adressen (und daraus abgeleitete ungefähre Standorte), Geräte-/Werbekennungen, Interaktionen mit der App bzw. Werbung sowie Diagnosedaten verarbeiten. Zwecke können die Anzeigenauslieferung, Messung, Betrugsprävention und – soweit zulässig – personalisierte Werbung sein. Details hängen von Region, Einwilligung und SDK-Konfiguration ab.

Weitere Informationen: Datenschutzerklärung von Google und Datenerfassung durch das Mobile Ads SDK.

Eine Ablehnung personalisierter Werbung bedeutet nicht zwangsläufig, dass keinerlei Werbung angezeigt wird.
